     Formally prove in Lean 4 that matrix operations transforming RGB signals across color primaries (Rec.709, Rec.2020, ACES) are invertible, orthogonal, and mathematically incapable of yielding negative luminance values or out-of-gamut coordinate inversions under IEEE-754 floating-point constraints.

342. **Deterministic Shader Worst-Case Execution Time (WCET) Bounds**  
     Shaders are verified in Lean 4 to guarantee that loop iterations, ray-traversal depth limits, and mathematical evaluations terminate within a deterministic cycle count, ensuring that rendering pipelines cannot experience frame-rate drops under pathological scene complexity.

343. **Floating-Point Precision Boundedness in BVH Traversal**  
     Prove using interval arithmetic in Lean 4 that ray-box intersection algorithms (e.g., the slabs method) cannot suffer from catastrophic cancellation or division-by-zero errors when rays are parallel to coordinate axes, guaranteeing that rays will never pass through solid geometry undetected.

344. **Zero-Latency Direct Sensor-to-Photon Hardware Loop**  
     Sample mouse, keyboard, and tracking sensors directly inside the display engine’s vertical-blanking or scanline interrupt. The view matrix is updated and injected into the scanout reprojection engine nanoseconds before the pixels hit the physical HDMI/DisplayPort cable.

345. **Silicon-Level Self-Healing Display Recovery (Sub-Millisecond TDR)**  
     If a GPU hardware warp faults or encounters an unrecoverable memory exception, the isolated OS scheduler isolates the faulty compute unit, cleans up its memory allocation, and restores the display state from the rolling scanline buffer in under 1 millisecond without an OS crash or screen flickering.

346. **Hardware-Enforced Temporal Anti-Aliasing (TAA) Jitter Invariants**  
     Sub-pixel projection offsets (Halton sequences, Sobol sequences) are generated by hardware PRNGs locked to the frame-clock index. Lean 4 proofs ensure that the sequence is uniformly distributed across the pixel square, eliminating temporal ghosting and crawling artifacts by construction.

347. **Formal Verification of Tone-Mapping Operators**  
     Prove in Lean 4 that non-linear tone-mapping functions (Reinhard, ACES Filmic, Uncharted 2) are monotonic and strictly bounded:
     $$\forall L \in [0, \infty), \quad \frac{d}{dL} \text{ToneMap}(L) \ge 0 \quad \text{and} \quad 0 \le \text{ToneMap}(L) \le 1$$
     guaranteeing that extreme exposure values will never cause high-contrast color banding, numerical overflow, or luminance inversions.

     Evaluate tone curves, HDR luminance clamping, and dithering using vectorized polynomial approximations executed with branchless Fused Multiply-Add (FMA) instructions, ensuring that visual post-processing consumes identical cycle budgets across all scenes.

     Bloom and lens-flare convolutions are formulated as physical optical scattering operations through the lens aperture. The convolution filter kernels are normalized at the hardware register level, ensuring that applying optical glare cannot increase the total radiant flux of the output frame.
